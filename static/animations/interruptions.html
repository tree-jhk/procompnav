Interrupt Often vs. Ask Once

Someone is working at their desk while a home robot searches the house for their cabinet. Same robot, same three questions. Only the timing changes. When should it ask?

STUDY 2 · QUESTION DELIVERY · 20 PARTICIPANTS

Asked to choose, most people picked asking once.

Why: each interruption pulls attention away from the task; batching questions means fewer interruptions (Bailey & Konstan 2006; Fitz et al. 2019).

So ProCompNav explores and collects candidates first, then asks its few questions back-to-back.

Focus curves and meters in the timelines are illustrative, not measured data. Question times follow the Study 2 setup.
