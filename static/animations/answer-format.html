Describe It vs. Just Say Yes

The same person, the same cabinet, the same robot asking two questions. One robot asks them to describe it; the other asks yes/no. Which one would you rather answer?

STUDY 1 · RESPONSE FORMAT · 20 PARTICIPANTS

Every participant preferred yes/no.

Real people answer open questions briefly: 89.1% of the Korean open-ended responses contained at most three words. Simulated users write long answers instead.

On CoIN-Bench Val Seen, simulated users wrote 460.2 response tokens per episode for open-ended questions (Pooled Independent Matching) vs 4.2 with ProCompNav. Yes/no questions keep real and simulated users aligned.

So ProCompNav asks short yes/no questions: one tap per answer.

Effort meters and curves are illustrative, not measured. The open-ended example questions and answers are illustrative; the yes/no questions follow the paper's running example. Answer durations use the Study 1 medians.
